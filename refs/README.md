# Reference material

Source material for the thesis. The PDFs are kept locally and are not committed, since
`*.pdf` is gitignored repo-wide. This index records what each one is and which concepts
it is the reference for.

Filenames follow `firstauthor_year`. For arXiv preprints the year is the year of the
arXiv identifier rather than of any later publication.

Entries here stay at pointer level. Claim-level detail, derivations, and what a paper
does and does not establish belong in the study at `refs/<key>.md`, beside the PDF. This
file is grepped rather than read, so it stays cheap.

Use the `refs` skill to add a paper.

## Masking and sampling

### `efraimidis_2006.pdf`

Efraimidis and Spirakis, "Weighted random sampling with a reservoir", *Information
Processing Letters* 97(5), 181-185, 5 pp. DOI 10.1016/j.ipl.2005.11.003.

Weighted sampling without replacement by drawing one key per item and taking the largest
keys. The reference for the exponential-race key construction, for its reservoir and
exponential-jumps forms (A-Res and A-ExpJ), and for the equivalence between key-order and
successive weight-proportional selection, which is the Plackett-Luce ordering under
another name.

Elsevier copy. Text extracts, but with an intra-word spacing artefact that needs
collapsing.
